roost bridge

Lets a Claude running in roost use this browser. Every Claude session asks you once.

The roost pages that may relay calls, e.g. https://roost.example.com or http://127.0.0.1:8444.

    Run roost browser-key on the roost host and paste what it prints. Calls not signed with it are refused.

    One domain per line (subdomains included). Applies in unsafe mode too.

    Pages Claude opens go into a "Claude" tab group in your window. On, each one comes to the front as it opens, so you can watch. Off, they open behind your tab, and a screenshot shows one only for a moment before handing your tab back.

    Every call from every roost Claude is allowed with no prompt. The toolbar badge shows !! while it is on.
